User Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how users and workspace owners can request deletion of personal data or platform data connected to DirotiQ CRM, including data received through Meta, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn integrations.

How to Request Deletion

Email us at mthokozisigatsheni89@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Please include the email address used in DirotiQ CRM, your workspace or business name, and the connected platform account if relevant.

What We Delete

  • CRM user profile data connected to the requesting account, where deletion is permitted.
  • Connected social account tokens and integration records.
  • Platform data received from Meta or LinkedIn that is no longer required to provide the service.
  • Leads, messages, posts, analytics, or campaign records when requested by an authorized workspace administrator.
