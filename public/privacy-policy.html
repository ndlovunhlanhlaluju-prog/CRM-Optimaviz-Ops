DirotiQ CRM

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DirotiQ CRM ("DirotiQ", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when people use our customer relationship management platform and connected services.

DirotiQ CRM processes customer and lead information on behalf of each workspace. Workspace administrators remain responsible for having an appropriate legal basis to collect and use information they enter or connect to the platform.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how the service is used, we may process:

  • Account details such as name, email address, profile information, role, and workspace membership.
  • CRM records such as lead names, business details, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, tasks, pipeline activity, and custom fields.
  • Communications data, including email and WhatsApp message metadata and content when a user connects and uses those services.
  • Integration details and authorization tokens required to maintain user-requested connections.
  • Website lead-capture and analytics information submitted through connected forms, webhooks, or tracking scripts.
  • Technical and security data such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, authentication events, and service logs.
  • Subscription and payment status. Payment credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider and are not stored as complete card details by DirotiQ CRM.

2. Meta and WhatsApp information

When a user chooses to connect a Meta or WhatsApp Business account, DirotiQ CRM requests only the permissions needed to complete that connection and provide messaging features. These may include public_profile, whatsapp_business_management, and whatsapp_business_messaging.

We may receive the user's Meta identifier and basic profile information, WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, message content and status events, templates, and access tokens. We use this information only to connect the selected business account, send or receive authorized business messages, display delivery status, maintain CRM communication history, and troubleshoot the integration. DirotiQ CRM does not sell Meta or WhatsApp user data.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve DirotiQ CRM.
  • Authenticate users and enforce workspace and brand access permissions.
  • Store and organize leads, tasks, communications, and business workflows.
  • Run integrations and actions expressly configured by authorized users.
  • Provide customer support, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and meet legal obligations.
  • Measure service performance and communicate important account or service updates.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information necessary with infrastructure, database, authentication, communications, analytics, and payment service providers that help operate DirotiQ CRM. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect people or the service, or during a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Information may also be shared with other authorized members of the same workspace, according to the roles and brand permissions configured by the workspace administrator.

5. Data retention

We retain information while an account or workspace is active and for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain appropriate business records. Connected integration tokens are removed or invalidated when the connection is disconnected where supported. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being securely overwritten.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls, workspace isolation, encrypted network connections, protected credentials, input validation, monitoring, and rate limiting. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, people may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of their personal information, or object to certain processing. Users can disconnect Meta or WhatsApp from the applicable integration settings and may also remove DirotiQ CRM from their Meta business integrations.

8. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of information associated with DirotiQ CRM, email ndlovunhlanhlaluju@gmail.com with the subject DirotiQ CRM Data Deletion Request. Include the account email, workspace name, and the connected Meta or WhatsApp Business Account name, if applicable. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will verify the request, remove or anonymize information that we are not legally required to retain, and confirm completion. Workspace end users may need to contact their workspace administrator first because that organization controls the CRM records it collected.

9. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country. Where required, we use appropriate contractual or legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Children

DirotiQ CRM is a business service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through personal consumer accounts.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service, law, or our practices. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
